Internal reference · not linked from the site

Type & style sheet

Every text style currently running on luminhuei.com, rendered in the real fonts at the real sizes. Sizes written as clamp(min, fluid, max) scale with the window — the px in brackets is what you get on a 1440 px-wide desktop.

Two files hold all of it: styles.css (home, work index, about) and case.css (every case study). The tokens below are defined once in :root and reused everywhere.

01

The three typefaces

Display · --serif

Instrument Serif

Google Fonts · OFL
Regular 400 + Italic
Fallback: Noto Serif TC, Georgia

Aa — Bb

The voice of the site. Every headline, section title, big number and the footer signature. Only one weight exists (400) — emphasis comes from italic and from size, never from bold.

Text & UI · --sans

Geist

Google Fonts · OFL (Vercel)
400 / 500 / 600 / 700
Fallback: Noto Sans TC, system UI

Aa — Bb

Everything you read for information: body copy, nav, buttons, metadata values, project names. 400 for paragraphs, 500 for UI, 600 for names and headings inside cards.

Label · --mono

Geist Mono

Google Fonts · OFL
400 / 500
Fallback: ui-monospace

Aa — Bb

Small uppercase labels only: kickers, metadata keys, captions, years, counts. Always tracked out (+0.06 to +0.08em) and never used above 14 px.

Chinese. The Latin fonts have no CJK glyphs, so 繁中 automatically falls through to Noto Serif TC (display) and Noto Sans TC (text). Three overrides live at the bottom of styles.css: body line-height goes 1.5 → 1.75, headline tracking goes negative → +0.02em (CJK hates tight tracking), and <em> drops italic for grey, because Chinese has no italics. The homepage headline is the one exception — it uses the handwriting face 辰宇落雁體 as an accent.

The rule of thumb. Serif says what the page is about. Sans says what you need to know. Mono says where you are. If a new element does not clearly belong to one of those three jobs, it probably does not need new type — it needs one of the sizes above.
