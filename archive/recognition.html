Record

The work has been to more countries than I have.

Eight A’ Design Awards between 2020 and 2024 — starting with posters and websites from grad school, then the brand, interior and web systems I built for Gong cha’s US market. The pieces travelled after that: fourteen exhibitions, from a design museum in Como to design weeks in Shenzhen and Xi’an, and architecture festivals in Mumbai and New Delhi.
